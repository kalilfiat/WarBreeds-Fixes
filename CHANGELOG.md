# Changes

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-14

- First portable community package, with independent feature selection and 24 executable combinations.
- Optional widescreen; wheel zoom depends on it. Original gameplay aspect ratio can be retained.
- Local backups, uninstall, integrity checks and rollback on caught write failures.
- Proportional cinematic canvas selected from the primary desktop at installation rather than fixed to the original tester's 4K display.
- Public and personal offline archives. Public archive acquires missing QuickTime 2 components separately and verifies their hashes.
- User confirms wheel zoom and movies. Brief black flash during resolution zoom remains a known limitation.
- Failed menu artwork experiments excluded.
